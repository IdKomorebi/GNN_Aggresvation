Measured on one otherwise idle GPU, retraining the attacker family sequentially for one field set takes 14--56\,s (the perceptrons dominate; one network per target), whereas the amortized estimate takes 62--78\,ms, a factor of 191--763. A complete $K=2$ table (all sets of size $\le3$) therefore takes 2--9\,min to scan, against 7--53\,h of sequential retraining; certification adds three retrained backgrounds per field and target, i.e.\ a few hundred sets. Pre-training a backbone takes about half a minute and is done once per data set. The savings compound over the recurrent workloads of an audit: re-grading after every release, redefined targets, and the sets of size four needed for $K=3$ disposal.
