All times below were measured on one otherwise idle GPU, on the same 200 random field sets per data set and with the same batch size. Retraining the attacker family sequentially for one field set takes 14--56\,s (the perceptrons dominate; one network per target). Our estimator takes 48--64\,ms per set, a factor of 241--906; a complete $K=2$ table (all sets of size $\le3$) therefore takes 1--7\,min to scan, against 7--53\,h of sequential retraining, and certification adds three retrained backgrounds per field and target, i.e.\ a few hundred sets. The time of a readout grows with the feature dimension and our estimator solves three of them, one per seed, so a one-seed estimator costs a third (16--21\,ms); Tables~\ref{tab:design} and~\ref{tab:baselines} list the time of every variant and alternative. Pre-training one reconstruction backbone takes 35--46\,s; three are trained once per data set, and the untrained networks need no training. The savings compound over the recurrent workloads of an audit: re-grading after every release, redefined targets, and the sets of size four needed for $K=3$.
